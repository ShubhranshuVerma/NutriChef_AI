# Food allergens

## Major allergens in the United States (FDA)
The Food Allergen Labeling and Consumer Protection Act (FALCPA, 2004) named eight major food allergens: milk, eggs, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat and soybeans. The FASTER Act added sesame as the ninth major allergen, effective 1 January 2023. Highly refined oils derived from these foods are exempt from FALCPA allergen labelling.

## Allergens that must be declared in India (FSSAI)
Under the Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Regulations, 2020, labels must declare foods and ingredients known to cause allergies: cereals containing gluten (wheat, rye, barley, oats, spelt or their hybrids), crustacean and their products, milk and milk products (including lactose), eggs and egg products, fish and fish products, peanuts, tree nuts and their products, soybeans and their products, and sulphite in a concentration of 10 mg/kg or more.

## Common hidden or regional names
- Milk: whey, casein, caseinate, lactose, ghee, butter, cream, malai, khoa/khoya, paneer, curd/dahi, buttermilk/chaas, lassi.
- Egg: albumin/albumen, mayonnaise, meringue; many cakes and baked goods.
- Wheat and gluten: atta, maida, suji/sooji/rava (semolina), dalia, seitan, most breads, pasta, noodles, vermicelli/sevai; soy sauce usually contains wheat.
- Soy: soya, tofu, tempeh, edamame, miso, soy sauce, soya chunks/granules, textured vegetable protein, soy lecithin (an emulsifier in chocolate and baked goods).
- Peanut: groundnut, moongphali, groundnut oil, peanut butter.
- Tree nuts: almond (badam), cashew (kaju), walnut (akhrot), pistachio (pista), pine nut (chilgoza); cashew paste is common in restaurant-style gravies.
- Sesame: til, tahini, gingelly oil.
- Crustaceans: prawn/jhinga, shrimp, crab, lobster.

## Safety notes
- Cross-contact can happen in kitchens, mills and factories even when an allergen is not an ingredient. "May contain" statements should be taken seriously.
- A recipe that avoids an allergen is not a guarantee of safety. People with serious allergies must read every label and confirm with a qualified health professional.
- When substituting, check that the replacement does not introduce another allergen (for example, replacing milk with almond milk introduces a tree nut; replacing paneer with tofu introduces soy).
