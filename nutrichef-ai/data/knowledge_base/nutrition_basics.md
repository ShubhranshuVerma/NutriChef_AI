# Nutrition basics

## Energy from macronutrients
Calories are estimated with the general Atwater factors: about 4 kcal per gram of protein, 4 kcal per gram of carbohydrate and 9 kcal per gram of fat (alcohol provides about 7 kcal per gram). NutriChef calculates nutrition from ingredient weights and USDA FoodData Central values per 100 g; it does not ask the LLM for numbers.

## Protein
Protein supports muscle and tissue repair. Good vegetarian sources include paneer, curd, milk, dals and other pulses, chickpeas, rajma, soy foods, nuts and seeds; eggs are a high-quality source for eggetarians. ICMR-NIN (2020) recommends about 0.83 g of protein per kg of body weight per day for healthy adults. "High-protein" in NutriChef is a relative target set by the user (for example at least 25-30 g in a meal).

## Fibre
Dietary fibre comes from whole grains, pulses, vegetables, fruits, nuts and seeds. The FDA Daily Value for dietary fibre is 28 g per day on a 2,000 kcal reference diet.

## Balancing a plate
A balanced Indian meal often combines a cereal (rice, roti, millets), a pulse or other protein, vegetables and a small amount of fat. Cooking oil and ghee add calories quickly: a tablespoon of oil is roughly 13-14 g of fat.

## Important limits
Nutrition values are estimates. Actual values vary with variety, ripeness, brand, cooking losses and portion size. NutriChef supports general wellness planning and is not medical advice; people with medical conditions should follow a doctor's or registered dietitian's guidance.
