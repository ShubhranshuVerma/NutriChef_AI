# Ingredient substitutions

Every substitution must be re-checked against the user's allergies, exclusions and diet. The notes below say what each swap introduces.

## Dairy
- Paneer -> tofu (firm): similar texture and protein, but introduces **soy**. Paneer -> boiled chickpeas or soya chunks for curries (soya chunks are **soy**).
- Milk -> oat milk (may contain **gluten** unless certified gluten-free), almond milk (**tree nut**), soy milk (**soy**), coconut milk (richer, more fat).
- Curd -> coconut yogurt for vegan diets; lower in protein.
- Cream in gravies -> blended curd (dairy), cashew paste (**tree nut**), or blended boiled onion with a little oil (no common allergens).
- Ghee -> neutral vegetable oil for vegan diets.
- Whey protein -> pea protein or extra pulses (dal, chickpeas, sprouts); avoid soy protein if soy is restricted.

## Eggs
- Binding: 1 tablespoon ground flaxseed mixed with 3 tablespoons water replaces one egg in baking.
- Scrambles: paneer bhurji (dairy) or tofu bhurji (soy) replace egg bhurji.
- Chillas made from besan or moong dal give an egg-free, high-protein breakfast.

## Wheat and gluten
- Atta or maida -> besan, rice flour, or millet flours (jowar, bajra, ragi). Blends usually need binding because they lack gluten.
- Semolina (suji) upma -> poha or broken millet upma.
- Soy sauce -> tamari labelled gluten-free (still **soy**).

## Soy
- Tofu -> paneer (dairy) or chickpeas.
- Soya chunks -> paneer, chickpeas, rajma or mushrooms.
- Soy sauce -> a little salt, lemon and tamarind for savoury depth.

## Nuts and seeds
- Peanuts in poha -> roasted chana dal or sunflower seeds.
- Cashew paste -> melon seed (magaz) paste or blended curd.
- Sesame oil -> groundnut oil introduces **peanut**; use sunflower or mustard oil instead.
